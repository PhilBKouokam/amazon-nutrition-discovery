# Media, marks and evidence

Amazon and the product brands named in fixtures/source notes are third-party marks. They are used to identify the observed retailer and product examples; no employment, endorsement, sponsorship or official collaboration is claimed. The corresponding retailer, manufacturers and other rightsholders retain their marks, packaging artwork, source imagery and website content. Public availability of a URL does not establish permission to redistribute its contents.

This source package includes **no original raster media**. Product photos, downloaded label images and all screenshot evidence are omitted. Referenced image slots use neutral SVG placeholders authored for this export, with original aspect ratios. Placeholders are explicitly labeled and contain no original pixels. The preview notice distinguishes source-package placeholders from the original evidence described by the walkthrough. The original live Site and local history are unchanged.

`MEDIA-SOURCES.json` attributes each omitted original filename using public source links retained from fixture/source documents where established. It includes an original-file hash for matching a rights-cleared copy, not the file itself. Some exact image URLs were not recorded in the runtime fixture; use its attributed product or manufacturer page to identify the exact variant and packaging, and do not substitute a different formulation. User-supplied screenshot originals are excluded, and their original device filenames are not copied into this provenance index.

To restore images locally after establishing permission, obtain the exact recorded public image or a separately cleared copy, inspect the current label/variant, and retain the historical fixture date rather than claiming a new verification. For screenshots, open the recorded search in an authorized browser and produce a new privacy-safe capture, or obtain a rights-cleared existing crop from the owner. The historical screenshot cannot be reproduced from a search URL alone; no original user screenshot is downloadable from this repo. Confirm identity, delivery location, account, cart and metadata removal before any distribution.

Place acquired originals in an ignored `original-media/` folder. Replace the corresponding SVG filename references in HTML/JS/fixtures only when you intentionally restore them. Recheck attribution, permission, local links and privacy before a public commit. Automated retrieval or hotlinking is not enabled. Original product/label images, BEFORE captures and the Walmart row reference will not ship with this repository candidate; the live walkthrough remains the visual reference.
